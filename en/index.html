Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 26, 2026

Oh my Photos! is developed by Asiel Posada. This policy explains how the app handles information on your device, and what happens when you visit this website or contact us.

Privacy contact: hello.bybinni@gmail.com.

Your photos and progress stay with you.

The app has no backend servers, requires no account, and does not send your photos or in-app activity data to the developer. It works offline with photos available on your device. Apple’s iCloud services, this website, and emails you choose to send are explained separately below.

1. Photo access and its purpose

With your permission, the app uses Apple’s Photos framework to read and display your photos and relevant library information, such as photo identifiers, dates, favorite status, media categories, albums, and album membership. This lets you review memories, organize albums, and select unwanted photos.

Full photo-library access is required for the app’s album features and memories modes, including date, favorites, screenshots, and album modes. Selected-photo access does not enable these features. Photo and album changes are made through Apple Photos; the app does not upload your library to the developer.

The app does not request device-location permission or track your live location.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the information needed to remember your progress and choices: onboarding status, preferences, identifiers of staged photos, album preferences and frame assignments, review bookmarks, gems, frame inventory, and local activity records. Photo identifiers refer to items in your library; they are not copies of your photos.

Your original photos remain in Apple Photos. The app may load and temporarily cache images for display. App records are kept in local storage; there is no developer-operated cloud database or account synchronization.

3. Local activity statistics

The app records activity times, event types, counts, and favorite status to show your progress and statistics. Events include sessions, reviews, keeps, revisits, staging photos, restoring staged photos, and completed deletions.

These statistics contain no photo content, photo identifiers, or album names. They remain on your device and are not transmitted to the developer. The app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or a third-party analytics service, and we do not sell your photos or activity data.

4. Photo deletion and your choices

Putting a photo in the app’s virtual trash only stages it; it does not delete the original. Photo deletion requires confirmation in Apple’s system prompt. Removing a photo from the app’s virtual trash restores it to the app’s available photos without deleting it from your library.

Deleted photos may remain in Apple Photos’ Recently Deleted album, subject to Apple’s rules. If iCloud Photos is enabled, photo and album changes, including deletion, may sync to your other devices. Deleting this app does not undo changes you already confirmed in Apple Photos.

You can change or revoke photo access in your device’s Settings at any time. Revoking access stops the photo features, but does not erase app records already stored on the device.

5. Retention and removing app data

Local records remain until the app removes them during normal use or you delete the app. To remove the app’s local data, use Delete App in your device’s Settings; offloading the app preserves its documents and data. Deleting the app does not delete your Apple Photos library.

Device or iCloud backups may retain app data and restore it later. You can manage those backups through Apple’s settings. We cannot remotely access, retrieve, or delete records that exist only on your device or in your backups.

6. Offline use, Apple services, and Rive

No app account or internet connection is needed to use photos already available on your device. If an original is stored only in iCloud, Apple Photos needs an internet connection to download it. Opening this online policy or sending email also requires a connection.

Apple handles Photos permissions, iCloud Photos, backups, and any system diagnostics according to your settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

The app uses the Rive runtime to display animations bundled with the app. We do not send your photos or activity data to Rive. We do not share that data with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Contacting us

If you email hello.bybinni@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever information or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to answer your request and provide support. Please avoid including private photos or other sensitive information unless needed for your request.

Our contact mailbox is hosted by Gmail, which processes messages under Google’s privacy policy. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence by emailing the same address; we will explain any legal reason that prevents deletion.

Where we rely on your consent to handle information you choose to send us, you can withdraw that consent by contacting us. This does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew consent.

8. This privacy website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, cookies, tracking scripts, external fonts, or forms. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without a GitHub account. See GitHub Pages’ data-collection disclosure and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Visiting this website does not give it access to your photo library or local app records. If you follow an external link, that service’s privacy policy applies to your visit.

9. Children’s information

The app does not require an account or ask for a date of birth. The local photo and activity handling described in this policy also applies when a child uses the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support email, contact us so we can review and delete that correspondence, subject to applicable legal obligations.

10. Security and incident notices

Photo access is controlled by iOS permissions, and the app’s records are kept in local app storage. Keeping these records on your device limits the information available to the developer. No device, storage system, or method of communication is completely secure.

If we discover a security incident affecting personal information you have sent us, we will notify affected people and authorities when required by applicable law.

11. Privacy requests and policy updates

For privacy questions or to exercise rights available under applicable law, contact hello.bybinni@gmail.com. For information that stays on your device, use the permission and deletion controls described above; the developer has no copy to provide or erase.

We will update this page and its date when our privacy practices change. If a future feature introduces new data collection or sharing, we will explain it and obtain any required permission before it begins.

Earlier versions are available in this policy’s public revision history. You can also request a previous version by email.
