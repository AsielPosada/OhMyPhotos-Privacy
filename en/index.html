Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Oh my Photos! is developed by Asiel Posada. The app requires no account and has no backend servers.

1. Data and purpose

With your permission, the app reads photos and library information (identifiers, dates, favorites, media types and albums) to display memories, organize albums and select photos for deletion. These features require full photo access.

The app stores preferences, progress, photo references and activity records (actions, times, counts and favorite status) locally to remember your choices and show usage statistics.

2. Sharing and online services

The app does not send photos or activity records to the developer or third-party SDK providers. It has no advertising or cross-app tracking.

You can use locally available photos offline. iCloud Photos, cloud downloads and device backups are handled by Apple according to your settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Retention and your controls

Local records remain until removed by the app or until you delete it. Use Delete App, rather than Offload App, to remove its local data; manage backups separately in your device settings.

You can revoke photo access in Settings. This stops photo access but does not erase existing local records. Deleting the app does not delete your photo library. Staging photos does not delete them; library deletion requires your confirmation in the system prompt. Library changes may sync through iCloud Photos.

4. Website and contact

GitHub Pages hosts this policy and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add website analytics.

If you contact hello.bybinni@gmail.com, your email address and message contents are processed through Gmail (Google’s privacy policy) to respond. Correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. Use the same email for privacy questions or to request deletion.
