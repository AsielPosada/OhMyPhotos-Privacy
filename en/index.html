Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Oh my Photos! is developed by byBinni! You can use the app without creating an account.

1. Data and purpose

With your permission, the app reads photos and library information (identifiers, dates, favorites, media types and albums) to display memories, organize albums and select photos for deletion. These features require full photo access.

The app stores preferences, progress, photo references and activity records (actions, times, counts and favorite status) locally to remember your choices and show usage statistics.

When you tap “Show in widget” on a framed photo, the app saves a thumbnail on your device so the widget can display it with the frame you chose. The app and widget share the thumbnail, the photo’s library identifier, your frame choice and dates used to keep the image up to date. The app marks this data for exclusion from device backups.

2. Subscriptions and online services

Apple handles subscription billing. We use RevenueCat to verify subscriptions, restore purchases and manage access. RevenueCat receives App Store transaction data, a randomly generated app user ID, and app and device information, including a device identifier when available. The app also sends RevenueCat which subscription offers you see so we can measure their performance, even if you never buy a subscription.

RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf in the United States. We require RevenueCat to protect it to the same standard as this policy under our data processing agreement. Photo and gameplay records stay on your device. The app has no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Apple handles iCloud Photos, cloud downloads and device backups according to your settings and Apple’s privacy policy.

3. Retention and your controls

The app keeps one widget photo at a time. Choosing another replaces the previous thumbnail. Removing the widget from your Home Screen keeps your selection in the app. To clear the saved thumbnail and its details, open the selected photo in the app and tap “Remove from widget”. Your original photo stays in your library.

Local records remain until removed by the app or until you delete it. Use Delete App, rather than Offload App, to remove its local data; manage backups separately in your device settings.

We keep subscription and offer-view records in RevenueCat as long as needed to manage subscriptions, measure offer performance or meet legal obligations. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

You can revoke photo access in Settings. This stops photo access but does not erase existing local records. Deleting the app does not delete your photo library. Staging photos does not delete them; library deletion requires your confirmation in the system prompt. Library changes may sync through iCloud Photos.

4. Website and contact

GitHub Pages hosts this policy and logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add website analytics.

If you contact holahola.bybinni@outlook.com, your email address and message contents are processed through Outlook (Microsoft’s privacy statement) to respond. Correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete personal data, object to or limit its use or sharing, or withdraw consent where applicable, use the same email. Describe your request so we can verify it and respond.
